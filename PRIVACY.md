# SPYRO Automation - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

SPYRO Automation is a personal business automation project run by one person for his own use. It is not a public service.

## What the app accesses
The app connects to the owner's own Google account (spyro.automation.core@gmail.com) to read and write rows in the owner's own Google Sheets. It uses this only to store and process the owner's business leads.

## What we do with data
- Data stays in the owner's own Google Sheets and own systems.
- We do not sell, share or give data to any third party.
- We do not use Google user data for advertising.
- We do not collect data from any other users.

## Data removal
The owner can remove the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page and can delete the data directly in Google Sheets.
